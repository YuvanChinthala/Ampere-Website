      Before you file

      Why is one week the shortest hire?+

      A canopy has to be moved, placed, earthed and collected. That mobilisation is priced into seven days minimum.

      Why does the hire stop at one month?+

      Ampere is temporary standby. Thirty days is the longest docket this desk will write.

      Can I pay, or will a generator arrive?+

      No. This is a sample website. Filing stores a docket on this device only. Nothing is charged or dispatched.

      What does VIP change?+

      While VIP is marked, machine hire is 8% lower and city delivery is waived. ₹19 is listed, not billed.
